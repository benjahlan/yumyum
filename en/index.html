Release status

The Android app is being prepared for wider testing. Google sign-in setup and real-device acceptance checks are still in progress. This website is the app’s information and privacy site; it does not accept orders or provide an app download.

Your information, your choices.

Read what is stored, who can see it and how to remove your account in our Privacy Policy. Account deletion instructions are available without signing in.

Does YumYum deliver food or process payments?

No. The current app supports community posts and pickup arrangements. Providers and customers arrange payment directly. YumYum does not verify food safety.

What is visible without an account?

Only approved posts explicitly enabled for public browsing. Private pickup details and review evidence are not included in the guest feed.

How do I get help?

Email doktosam@gmail.com. Never send passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.
